# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Adidas sales performance across different **time periods, regions, states, products, retailers, and sales methods**.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive KPIs and visualizations, helping users understand **sales trends, profitability, product performance, and regional performance**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across different states and regions.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze retailer-wise sales performance.
- Compare sales channels such as **In-store, Online, and Outlet**.
- Analyze performance across **2020 and 2021**.
- Provide an interactive and easy-to-understand business dashboard.

---

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard provides the following key performance indicators:

- 💰 **Total Sales:** $899.90M
- 📈 **Total Profit:** $332.13M
- 📦 **Total Units Sold:** 18M
- 💵 **Average Price per Unit:** $45.22
- 📊 **Profit Margin:** 42.30%

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
A monthly trend analysis showing how Adidas sales changed throughout the year and helping identify high- and low-performing months.

### 2. State-wise Sales
A geographical visualization showing sales performance across different states in the United States.

### 3. Region-wise Sales
A donut chart representing sales contribution from different regions such as:

- West
- Northeast
- Southeast
- South
- Midwest
- East

### 4. Product-wise Sales
A ranking of products based on sales performance, helping identify the products generating the highest revenue.

### 5. Retailer-wise Sales
A comparison of major retailers based on sales, including:

- West Gear
- Foot Locker
- Sports Direct
- Kohl's
- Amazon
- Walmart

### 6. Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

- **Sales Method:** In-store, Online, Outlet
- **Year:** 2020, 2021
- **Month:** January – December
- **Reset Filter:** Quickly clear selections and return to the default dashboard view.
